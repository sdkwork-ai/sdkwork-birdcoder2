# Product Introduction PPT

Turn a product into a concise introduction deck that sells the value, not just the features.

## Workflow

1. Fix the brief: audience (customer/investor/partner), goal of the presentation, and length (default 10–15 slides, ~15 minutes).
2. Story arc: hook slide (the problem), product slide (what it is in one line), value slides (each feature mapped to a customer benefit), proof (usage/case), pricing/offer, and the call to action.
3. Per slide: one headline claim, one visual or a short list, and the spoken cue — feature names only where they support the claim.
4. Keep the demo story in mind: slides set up what the live demo shows, never duplicate it.
5. Deliver the slide-by-slide table (claim, content, visual, cue); produce the deck in the requested format when asked.

## Rules

- Every slide answers "so what" for the audience — a feature without its benefit is cut.
- The CTA slide states exactly the next step (contact/book a demo/buy) with the channel.
