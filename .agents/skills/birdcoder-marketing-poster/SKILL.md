# Marketing Poster

Produce an operations poster whose copy and layout serve one campaign goal.

## Workflow

1. Capture the campaign facts: offer/event, audience, channel (feed/story/print), deadline, and the action to take.
2. Copy blocks: hook headline, one-line value statement, offer details, urgency element (deadline/stock), and the CTA with its channel (QR, link, store).
3. Layout for the channel: safe margins, thumb-stopping top zone for feeds, readable-at-a-distance type for print.
4. Compliance check: prices, dates, and claim wording accurate and consistent across every block.
5. Deliver: copy sheet (per block), layout spec, and asset list (logo, QR, product shots) with sizes.

## Rules

- The CTA survives: if nothing else is remembered, the viewer must still know what to do next.
- One campaign per poster — split mixed messages into a series.
