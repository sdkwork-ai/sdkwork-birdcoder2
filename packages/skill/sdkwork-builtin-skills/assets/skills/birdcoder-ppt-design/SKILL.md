# Slide Deck Design

Turn a goal into a deck: story first, slides second, decoration last.

## Workflow

1. Define audience, decision/ takeaway, and slot length; derive the slide count (roughly 1–2 minutes per content slide).
2. Outline the story as claims — one claim per slide; the title states the claim, not the topic.
3. Per slide specify: headline (the claim), body (max three bullets or one visual), and the visual form (chart, diagram, image, quote).
4. Data slides: pick the chart by the comparison being made; label the takeaway on the slide, not in the notes.
5. Define the visual system once: fonts, colors, margins, and the grid every slide reuses.

## Deliverable

Slide-by-slide table: number, headline, body/visual, speaker-note seed. Then build the deck in the requested format when asked.
