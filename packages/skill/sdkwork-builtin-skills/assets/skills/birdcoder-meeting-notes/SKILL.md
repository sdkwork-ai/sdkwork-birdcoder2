# Meeting Notes

Convert a raw meeting record into notes people act on.

## Workflow

1. Capture the frame: meeting purpose, date, and attendees with roles.
2. Decisions first: every decision as one line with its owner and effective date; mark reversed earlier decisions.
3. Action items: verb-first task, owner, deadline, and the definition of done; nothing without an owner leaves the table.
4. Open questions: what is unresolved, who decides, and by when.
5. Optional per-topic detail after the summary; keep it scoped to what a non-attendee needs.

## Rules

- Attribute views to people; do not blend the room into "the team".
- Notes state outcomes and commitments — not a transcript of who said what in which order.
