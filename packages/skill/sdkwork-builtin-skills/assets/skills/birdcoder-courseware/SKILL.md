# Courseware Generation

Turn a lesson or topic into lecture-ready presentation slides.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the teaching context: audience (grade/level), lesson duration, and the key takeaways the lecture must land.
2. Outline the deck as claims — one claim per slide, ordered so each builds on the previous; ~1 slide per 1–2 minutes of lecture.
3. Per slide: title (the claim), 2–4 short bullets or one visual, and where an example or question slot belongs.
4. Speaker support: note under each slide what to say and when to pause for questions; call out the slide that opens and the one that closes.
5. Deliver the slide-by-slide table; produce the actual deck in the requested format when asked.

## Rules

- Slides aid the lecture, not replace it: dense slides get cut, not shrunk.
- Numbers and definitions on slides must match the spoken explanation exactly.
