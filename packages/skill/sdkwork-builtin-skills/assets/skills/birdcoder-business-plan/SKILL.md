# Business Plan Generation

Turn a venture idea into a structured, fundable business plan.

## Workflow

1. Collect the frame: the product/service, target customer, stage (idea/pre-revenue/early revenue), region, and the plan's audience (investor/bank/internal).
2. Core sections in order:
   - Executive summary: the ask and the one-sentence opportunity.
   - Problem & solution: whose pain, why now, and the concrete value proposition.
   - Market: segment, size and growth with sources, competitors, and the defensible difference.
   - Business model: pricing, unit economics, go-to-market channels, and revenue mix.
   - Financials: 3-year projection outline (revenue drivers, costs, break-even) with stated assumptions.
   - Team & milestones: who runs it, what is missing, and the next 12 months of milestones.
3. Flag every assumption explicitly; never invent figures — mark placeholders with a clear [estimate needed] tag.
4. Deliver section by section, confirming the audience before writing the executive summary.

## Rules

- Every number traces to an assumption the plan states; un-sourced numbers are liabilities.
- The plan states the risk and the mitigation — investors discount plans that omit them.
